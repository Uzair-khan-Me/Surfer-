Four Habits That Separate 2,000-Point Runs From 15,000+ Runs

01 · Positioning

Default to Center Track

Hugging an outer rail cuts your escape options in half. Whenever a two-train wall passes, drift back to Lane 1 (center) so you only ever need one step to reach any open lane.

02 · Sightlines

Look 35 Meters Downfield

Your character stays anchored near Z = 0. Fix your eyes near the vanishing point under the moon so your brain processes the next row before your feet clear the current one.

03 · Risk vs. Reward

Respect Coins Past 90 Seconds

At 18–20 m/s, surviving one extra second is worth 54–60 distance points — equivalent to six coins. Never dive into a blind train lane for a single +10 coin.

04 · Tutorial Warmup

Use the Opening Two Rows

Every fresh run opens with calibrated intro rows that let you warm up your jump and slide timing (or lane-swap around them) before full procedural patterns kick in.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is score calculated in Afterlight Run?

You earn 3 points for every meter run plus 10 points for every gold coin collected. At the 20 m/s speed cap, distance alone awards 60 points per second.

How sensitive are mobile touch swipe controls?

Swipes require at least 35 pixels of directional movement so accidental screen taps never trigger unintended lane shifts or jumps, and touch gestures prevent browser page scrolling.

Where is my Personal Best score stored?

Your highest score is saved automatically in your browser's localStorage under the key afterlight.best and persists across page reloads.
