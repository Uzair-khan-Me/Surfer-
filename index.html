About the game

Afterlight Run is a free neon-city endless runner you play right in the browser

Three lanes, one endless night. Afterlight Run drops you onto a railway that keeps building itself in front of you — dodge the traffic, pocket the light and see how far you get before the city finally wins.

The run starts slow and honest. You move forward on your own, and everything you control fits on four keys: left and right move you between three lanes, Space lifts you over orange barriers, and S drops you into a slide under blue gates. Trains are the real threat — they own a whole lane, they are far too tall to jump and too long to slide under, so the skill is reading the track two obstacles ahead and already being somewhere else when it arrives.

Nothing stands between you and the first run. There is no account, no download, no loading screen and no ad break between attempts: the game is a single web page, so it opens in about a second and restarts with one key press. Your personal best is stored in your own browser rather than on a server, and the page ships no analytics, no tracking cookies and no third-party requests.

Who Afterlight Run is for

  • Players who grew up on three-lane endless runners and want a sharper, faster, better looking version that runs in a browser tab.
  • Anyone on a work or study break who wants ninety seconds of focus without installing an app or creating an account.
  • Web developers and students who want to poke at a real WebGL 2 game: the source is public and every asset is generated in code.

Features

What makes this endless runner different

Endless and never twice the same

City blocks, traffic, barriers and gates are generated as you run, so no two attempts follow the same line.

Three lanes, three moves

Arrow keys or A and D on desktop, a swipe on touch screens. Left, right, jump, slide — that is the whole control scheme.

Smooth on real hardware

An adaptive renderer watches frame time and scales resolution, shadows and draw distance to hold a steady frame rate.

Zero friction

No sign-up, no install, no loading screen: the first frame arrives in under a second and restarting is one key press.

Distance, coins and personal bests

Every metre earns points and every coin adds ten. Your best run is saved on your own device.

Plays offline

Install it once as an app and the whole city keeps running with no connection at all.

Hand-built WebGL pipeline

Procedural geometry, pooled objects and bundled fonts instead of downloaded 3D models, textures or CDN requests.

Keyboard first, accessible by default

Every action has a key binding, controls are labelled for screen readers and reduced-motion preferences are respected.

How to play

How to play Afterlight Run in one minute

There is no tutorial level and nothing to unlock: press Let's Run, or hit Enter, and the game is already in motion. These are the only six things you need to know.

  1. Open the game and start a run

    Load the page and press Let's Run, or press Enter. The run starts immediately — there is no tutorial level to sit through.

  2. Switch between the three lanes

    Press the left or right arrow key, or A and D, to move between lanes. On a phone, swipe left or right.

  3. Jump over the orange barriers

    Press Space, W or the up arrow to jump a barrier, or change lane and leave it behind. On a phone, swipe up.

  4. Slide under the blue gates

    Blue overhead gates need you low to the ground: press S or the down arrow to slide, or move into a clear lane.

  5. Change lane to dodge the trains

    Trains fill a whole lane and are far too tall to jump, so read the track ahead and switch lanes early.

  6. Collect coins and score points

    Each coin adds ten points and the coin trails usually trace a safe route through the traffic.

  7. Beat your personal best

    Distance raises your score, the city keeps speeding up, and your best run is stored locally so the next attempt has a target.

Scoring

Scoring, speed and your personal best

Afterlight Run scores two things: how far you get and how much light you collect. The score climbs with every metre survived and jumps by ten for every coin, and because the city accelerates the longer you last, a late metre is worth more than an early one in practice — surviving the speed-up is what produces high scores.

Distance
Points accrue continuously as you run; the counter in the top left is your score.
Coins
Ten points each, and they ring up the coin counter on the right of the screen.
Speed
The world accelerates on its own schedule — the speed read-out (metres per second) shows how fast the track is moving.
Personal best
Stored in your own browser with local storage. No account, no leaderboard, no server round-trip.
Game over
One hit ends the run: an orange barrier, a blue gate or any part of a train. Press Enter or Space to run it back.

Play better

Pro tips from a lot of failed runs

  • Read two obstacles ahead instead of one — the pattern repeats, so react to the gap you are about to reach.
  • Jump early rather than late. Landing on top of a train ends the run, and a mistimed jump has no second chance.
  • Sliding keeps your hitbox low and your speed high, which makes it the safer read whenever a gate and a barrier share a lane.
  • Coin trails are a map: they are laid out along lines that are actually safe to run, so following them is rarely the wrong decision.
  • Hold the middle lane when you can. It keeps both escape routes open when a train appears without warning.
  • Play full screen on a desktop monitor — a taller viewport shows you the barriers earlier and buys you reaction time.

Questions

Afterlight Run: frequently asked questions

Everything people ask before their first run — controls, devices, offline play, scoring and what the game does with your data.

Is Afterlight Run free to play?

Yes. Afterlight Run is completely free, with no account, no in-app purchases, no ads and no paywall. Open the page and press Let's Run.

Do I have to download or install anything?

No. The game is a web page: it loads a few hundred kilobytes of HTML, CSS and JavaScript and starts in the browser. You can optionally install it to your home screen as a Progressive Web App, but nothing is required.

Which devices and browsers are supported?

Any modern browser with WebGL — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and their mobile versions on desktop, laptop, tablet or phone. Desktop players use the keyboard, touch devices use swipe gestures.

How do I control the runner?

You are always moving forward. Press the left or right arrow key (or A and D) to switch lanes, Space, W or the up arrow to jump, and S or the down arrow to slide. On a touch screen, swipe left, right, up or down. Escape or P pauses the run.

How is the score calculated?

Every metre you survive adds points, every coin adds ten, and the city moves faster the longer you last — so later distance is worth more. Your personal best is stored locally in your browser.

Where is my high score saved?

On your own device, in browser local storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and clearing site data resets the record.

Does Afterlight Run work offline?

Yes. After your first visit a service worker caches the game, so you can install it to your home screen and keep playing with no connection.

Are there ads, cookies or trackers?

None. There are no ad SDKs, no third-party analytics and no tracking cookies. Fonts, icons and artwork are bundled with the game instead of being fetched from a CDN.

What should I do when a train blocks my lane?

Change lane. Trains are too tall to jump and too long to slide under, so read the track ahead and move early — the middle lane usually keeps both escape routes open.

Is there a difficulty setting?

There is no menu setting to change: every run starts slow and gets harder by itself as speed and obstacle density ramp up. An adaptive renderer also adjusts resolution, shadows and draw distance to keep the frame rate smooth on your hardware.

What is Afterlight Run built with?

TypeScript, Three.js and Vite, rendered through WebGL 2. The city, the runner and every prop are generated procedurally in code, so there are no downloaded 3D models or texture files.

Can it be played with a keyboard only?

Yes. Every action has a keyboard binding, menus are reachable with Tab, buttons carry accessible labels and the interface honours the prefers-reduced-motion setting. Touch controls are a parallel input path, not a replacement.

Is it safe to run on school or shared computers?

Afterlight Run is single-player, with no chat, no accounts, no external requests and no data collection. The only thing it stores is your own high score, which stays in your browser.
