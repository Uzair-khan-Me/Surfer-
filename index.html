ABOUT AFTERLIGHT RUN

Afterlight Run is a free 3D three-lane endless runner browser game built with Three.js and a deterministic 120 Hz physics loop. Sprint down nocturnal transit tracks, dodge oncoming subway trains, leap orange barriers, slide beneath blue overhead gates, and collect gold coin lines — with zero downloads, zero accounts, and zero ads.

Is Afterlight Run completely free with no download?

Yes. Afterlight Run is 100% free to play directly in your web browser with no download, no installer, no account registration, and no ads or paywalls.

Is Afterlight Run similar to Subway Surfers?

Afterlight Run is an original indie 3D three-lane endless runner inspired by the classic arcade rhythm of transit-track runners like Subway Surfers. You switch across three railway lanes, dodge oncoming trains, jump low barriers, slide under overhead gates, and collect coin trails right in your browser without downloading a mobile app.

What devices and browsers support Afterlight Run?

Afterlight Run runs in any modern WebGL2 browser across Windows PCs, Macs, Chromebooks, Linux laptops, iPhones, iPads, and Android phones using adaptive quality scaling for smooth performance.

What are the keyboard and mobile touch controls?

On desktop and Chromebook, use A/D or Left/Right Arrows to switch lanes, Space/W/Up Arrow to jump, S/Down Arrow to slide, and P or Escape to pause. On phones and tablets, swipe left, right, up, or down anywhere on the screen.

Are obstacle patterns always fair and solvable?

Yes. Every procedural obstacle row is mathematically validated to contain at most two hazards and at least one guaranteed passable lane, with spacing calibrated so you always have time to cross lanes even at the 20 m/s maximum speed.
