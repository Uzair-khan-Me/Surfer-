Frequently Asked Questions

Does Afterlight Run run smoothly on Chromebooks and budget laptops?

Yes. Static city geometry is merged by material into ~120 draw calls and ~4.5 MB of geometry VRAM, while an automatic QualityManager adjusts pixel ratio and decorative detail across HIGH, MEDIUM, and LOW tiers if frame times rise.

Does switching browser tabs cause unfair crashes?

No. Afterlight Run automatically pauses your active run the moment you switch tabs or minimize the window, and waits until you explicitly resume.

Which browsers are supported across desktop and mobile?

Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Apple Safari (macOS and iOS), Firefox, Brave, and Opera on Windows, macOS, ChromeOS, Linux, iPhone, iPad, and Android.
