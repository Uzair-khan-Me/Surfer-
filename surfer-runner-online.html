Anatomy of the Midnight Transit Track

In high-speed train surfer and rail track runner games, visual readability determines whether a crash feels earned or unfair. Many browser runners overload the screen with clashing textures that hide hazards until the last frame. Afterlight Run engineers every visual layer around high-speed lane reading:

Transit Cars (Trains)

Hazard 02 · Jump or Shift

High-contrast striped barricades sit across the ballast bed. Vault straight over them with Space / W / ↑ or swipe up without losing lane alignment.
